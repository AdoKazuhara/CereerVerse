Quick guide

How to fix the plane

You do not need aviation knowledge. Follow the checklist and learn what each part does.

1
Check the fuel ⛽The plane needs enough fuel for the trip. If the gauge is low, refuel it.
2
Check the landing tyres 🛞Tyres must be inflated and ready to absorb the landing.
3
Check the engine 🔧Listen for an unusual rattle. A loose belt or part needs tightening.
4
Check the lights 💡Navigation lights help other pilots see the plane, especially at night.
5
Check the wing bolts 🪽The wings must be secure before takeoff. Tighten any loose bolts.

Every mission has a different mix of issues. You can inspect and repair them in any order—the goal is to understand the checklist, not guess one magic answer.
